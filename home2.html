Ding Repair & Shaping Process

From structural foam preps to high-grit glass polish — here is how we handle your board restoration.

01

Damage Inspection & Dry-Out

We examine the foam core for water logging and completely dry it out in our climate-controlled bay to prevent future delamination.

02

Foam Core Prep & Fill

Shattered glass is cut away and the core is rebuilt using matching density polyurethane or polystyrene foam inserts.

03

Fiberglass Lamination

Layers of premium aerial fiberglass cloth are laid down and laminated with matching polyester or epoxy resins for structural strength.

04

Hotcoating & Color Match

A sealing hotcoat is applied, followed by custom pigment sprays to match original color schemes, logos, and deck styles.

05

Sanding & Polishing

The repair is progressively hand-sanded from 120 up to 1500 grit, then machine-buffed for a completely seamless gloss finish.
