Our Story

Born from a love of the ocean and a dedication to the craft, Surfboard Workshop has been shaping dreams since 1998.

Born from a Love of Waves

Founded in 1998 by master shaper Jake Moreno, Surfboard Workshop began as a one-man shed operation in Malibu. Jake's obsession with the perfect board led to a workshop that now serves surfers from Malibu to Mavericks.

Every board that leaves our shop carries 25 years of accumulated knowledge, countless hours on the water, and a genuine passion for the craft of shaping.
