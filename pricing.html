Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our professional ding repair process, lead times, and materials.

How do you handle waterlogged surfboards?

If a surfboard is ridden after a ding has cracked the glass, the foam core will absorb seawater. We place waterlogged boards in our custom vacuum-drying chamber to extract 100% of the trapped moisture from the foam before sealing it. Repairing a damp board will seal moisture inside, leading to foam rot, yellowing, and eventual delamination.

Can you perfectly match custom resin tints and graphics?

Yes. Our ding repair technicians hand-blend pigments into our laminating resins to match standard and custom tints. We also perform foam staining for older boards. While absolute invisibility can depend on the age of the board and UV fading, we pride ourselves on making repairs virtually seamless and structurally superior.

Do you repair both Epoxy (EPS) and Polyester (PU) surfboards?

Absolutely. We use high-quality Polyester resins for standard PU boards, and premium Epoxy resins for EPS/Epoxy constructions. It is critical to use Epoxy resin on EPS boards because standard polyester resin has solvents that will instantly melt EPS polystyrene foam. Our workshop tracks board constructions carefully to ensure compatible materials are used.

What is your typical turnaround time?

Our standard repair turnaround is 2 to 3 business days, allowing for proper cure and sanding times. If you have a trip or an upcoming swell, we offer a Rush Service (24-Hour Turnaround) add-on for a surcharge of $80, subject to technician availability and drying requirements.
