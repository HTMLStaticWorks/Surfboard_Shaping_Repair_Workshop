Our Specialized Services

From custom shapes to precision repairs, we handle every board with artisan care.

Custom Shaping

Tailored board designs and hand-finished foam blanks crafted for your exact style and wave conditions.

Ding & Snap Repair

Professional resin work, structural reinforcements, and color matching to restore dings and snaps fast.

Glassing & Hotcoating

High-quality fiberglass lamination and UV-stable resins for maximum strength, flex, and weight.

Fin Box Rebuilds

Precision routing and installation of FCS II, Futures, and traditional longboard single fin boxes.

Vintage Restoration

Reviving historic, sun-damaged, or delaminated classic surfboards back to original surfing condition.

Shaping Consultation

1-on-1 sessions with a master shaper to design your board’s dimensions, foil, and rocker profile.
