Blank Selection

Choosing the right foundation for your performance.

Polyurethane (PU)

The industry standard for decades. Familiar flex, organic feel, and excellent glassing properties. Perfect for those who love the traditional surfing experience.

Expanded Polystyrene (EPS)

Lightweight and highly buoyant. EPS blanks combined with epoxy resin create a fast, responsive board ideal for progressive maneuvers and small wave performance.
