Blank Selection Guide

The right blank determines everything about your board's flex, weight, and durability.

Traditional

PU / Polyester

The classic combination. Familiar flex pattern, easy to glass, and affordable.

Performance

EPS / Epoxy

Lighter and more buoyant. Ideal for small waves and progressive surfers.

Eco

Bio-Epoxy

Sustainable blanks with plant-based resins — performance without compromise.
